The bundled static-basemap.jpg is a projected regional map image rendered from Natural Earth 1:10m Physical Land and Admin 0 Boundary Lines, version 5.1.1.

Natural Earth raster and vector map data are in the public domain. The JPEG displays simplified land, sea, and country boundaries. It contains no road network or place labels; country and sea names are rendered separately by the application. Sources: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-physical-vectors/ and https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/.
